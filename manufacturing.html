Vedic Method

5 Steps to Pure Golden Ghee

Step 01

Fresh A2 Cow Milk

Fresh, unprocessed A2 milk is collected daily from free-grazing Indian indigenous cows.

Step 02

Curd Formulation

Whole milk is boiled gently and converted overnight into rich, natural curd using traditional cultures.

Step 03

Wooden Bilona Churning

Curd is hand-churned bi-directionally using wooden bilona churners to extract pure makkhan (butter).

Step 04

Slow Heating

Makkhan is slowly melted on low flame over cow-dung cake fires to retain golden aroma and natural nutrients.

Step 05

Glass Jar Sealing

Pure golden liquid is filtered and poured into sterile glass jars where it naturally granulates into golden crystals.
